First Time Visiting The A&P? Here’s What To Expect When Shopping With Us

Visiting a cannabis dispensary for the first time can be an exciting experience, but it can also come with plenty of questions.

What should you bring? How does the process work? What products are available? What if you don’t know exactly what you’re looking for?

At A&P, we believe shopping for cannabis should feel straightforward, welcoming, and informative. Whether you’re an experienced cannabis consumer or stepping into a dispensary for the first time, our goal is to make it easier to understand your options and find products that fit your preferences.

Here’s what you can expect from your first visit.

Before You Visit

A little preparation can make your first dispensary visit even easier.

Before heading to A&P, take a moment to think about what you’re looking for. You don’t need to know the exact product or strain you want. Even having a general idea can help.

For example, you might be interested in:

  • Flower

  • Pre-rolls

  • Edibles

  • Vapes

  • Concentrates

  • CBD products

  • Other cannabis products

You may also want to consider whether you’re looking for something familiar or interested in exploring something new.

If you’re unsure, that’s perfectly fine. You can always ask our team for help understanding the differences between products.

Don’t Forget Your ID

One of the most important things to remember when visiting a dispensary is your identification.

Cannabis dispensaries are required to verify that customers meet the applicable legal age requirements before completing a purchase. Bring a valid, government-issued photo ID and have it ready when you arrive.

Requirements can vary depending on local regulations, so it’s always a good idea to make sure your identification meets the applicable requirements before your visit.

Step Inside and Start Browsing

Once you arrive, you’ll typically begin by checking in and verifying your eligibility to shop.

From there, you can explore the available products.

A dispensary menu can look a little different from a traditional retail store. Instead of simply seeing a product name and price, you’ll often see information about cannabinoids, potency, serving sizes, ingredients, product type, and more.

Don’t feel like you need to understand everything immediately.

Cannabis terminology can take some time to learn, and our team can help explain unfamiliar terms.

Understanding the Cannabis Menu

One of the biggest differences between a cannabis dispensary and other retail stores is the amount of information provided about each product.

You may see terms such as:

THC

THC is the cannabinoid most commonly associated with the intoxicating effects of cannabis.

CBD

CBD is another major cannabinoid that is not generally intoxicating in the same way THC is.

Potency

Potency describes the concentration or amount of cannabinoids in a product.

Serving Size

For products such as edibles, the serving size is particularly important because the total amount in a package may be divided into multiple servings.

Product Type

Cannabis can be available in several formats, including flower, pre-rolls, edibles, vapes, and concentrates.

Understanding these basics can make browsing much easier.

Not Sure What to Choose? Ask Questions

You don’t have to walk into A&P knowing exactly what you want.

In fact, asking questions is one of the best ways to make a more informed purchase.

You might ask:

“What’s the difference between these two products?”

“How much THC does this product contain?”

“How many servings are in this package?”

“What should I know about this product before purchasing it?”

“What’s the difference between flower and a pre-roll?”

These are all reasonable questions.

Our team can help you understand product information and compare options without making the process complicated.

Flower, Pre-Rolls, Edibles, and More

Cannabis products come in different formats, and each one offers a different way of consuming cannabis.

Flower

Cannabis flower is the traditional form of cannabis. It is sold in different strains, cultivars, cannabinoid profiles, and potency levels.

When browsing flower, you may encounter information about THC percentage, CBD content, aroma, and other characteristics.

Pre-Rolls

Pre-rolls are cannabis flower that has already been prepared for use.

They’re convenient for customers who want a ready-to-use option without preparing flower themselves.

Edibles

Edibles are cannabis-infused foods or beverages.

Because edible products are processed through the digestive system, their effects can differ from inhaled cannabis and may take longer to appear.

Always follow the product’s labeled serving instructions.

Vapes

Vape products allow cannabis extracts or other formulations to be inhaled through a compatible device.

Product composition and instructions can vary, so check the packaging carefully.

Concentrates

Concentrates contain cannabis compounds in a more concentrated form and can have significantly higher cannabinoid concentrations than traditional flower.

If you’re unfamiliar with concentrates, ask the dispensary team about the product and its labeled potency before making a purchase.

Take Your Time

There’s no need to rush your first dispensary visit.

If you’re comparing several products, take a moment to read their labels and understand what you’re purchasing.

Pay attention to:

  • THC content

  • CBD content

  • Serving size

  • Ingredients

  • Product type

  • Package size

  • Manufacturer information

  • Storage instructions

  • Any applicable warnings

The goal isn’t simply to find the product with the highest THC percentage.

Instead, think about what you’re comfortable with and what type of product you’re interested in exploring.

Completing Your Purchase

Once you’ve selected your products, you’ll head to checkout.

Your identification may be checked again as part of the purchase process, depending on the dispensary’s procedures and applicable regulations.

The staff can also answer any last-minute questions you have about the products you’ve selected.

Before leaving, make sure you understand the basic information on your products, including serving information and storage recommendations.

After You Get Home

Once you bring your cannabis products home, proper storage becomes important.

Keep products in their original packaging whenever possible and follow the manufacturer’s storage recommendations.

Cannabis products should be stored securely and kept away from children and pets.

For flower, protecting it from excessive light, heat, air, and moisture can help preserve its quality.

Your First Visit Doesn’t Have To Be Complicated

Your first dispensary experience should be about learning, exploring, and finding products you’re comfortable with.

You don’t need to know every cannabis term before walking through the door. You don’t need to have a favorite strain. And you certainly don’t need to feel embarrassed about asking questions.

At A&P, we’re here to help make the process easier.

Come in, explore your options, ask questions, and take your time.

Ready to Visit A&P?

Whether you already know what you’re looking for or you’re simply curious about your options, we’re here to help make your dispensary experience simple and approachable.

Explore our products and stop by A&P for your next visit.
